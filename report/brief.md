# Operations Planning Brief: Weekly Warehouse Flex Staffing Under Order-Line Uncertainty

**Prepared by:** Industrial Engineering & Operations Research Analytics  
**Scope:** Weekly Friday-origin allocation of 40 flexible labor hours (ten indivisible 4-hour blocks) across Monday–Friday picking operations  
**Empirical Dataset & Epistemic Boundary:** Evaluated on **1,067,371 raw transactions** (`992,034` deduplicated valid order-line picks across `105` complete Monday–Friday weeks, Dec 2009–Dec 2011) from [UCI Online Retail II (Dataset ID 502)](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii) ([DOI: 10.24432/C5CG6D](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D), CC BY 4.0). Invoice–SKU lines serve strictly as **proxies for potential order-picking demand**; physical pick rates, floor staffing, and WMS queue lengths are unobserved, and capacity parameters ($C, q$) are explicitly synthetic operational calibrations derived solely from pre-validation training data.

## 1. Operational Problem & Capacity Regime Diagnosis
Every Friday afternoon, fulfillment planners lock next week's flexible shift schedule—ten indivisible 4-hour blocks ($40\text{ hours/week}$, bounded at $0 \le x_d \le 4\text{ blocks/weekday}$)—before Monday–Friday orders arrive.

A critical capacity-planning finding emerges from the initial training split (`weeks 0..84`, Dec 2009–Jul 2011): because 7 of 8 training quarters fall in off-peak spring/summer (`median = 1,514 lines/day`), sizing Q4 staffing to the year-round P50 median ($C = 1{,}060$, $q = 227$, weekly capacity $7{,}570$ lines) yields **180% median utilization** on historical 2010 Q4 training weeks and saturates every single 2011 Q4 test week into the static front-loaded corner `4-4-2-0-0`—making forecasts irrelevant to staffing. Sizing peak-season capacity to the **initial-training 90th percentile (`Train P90 = 2,954 lines/day` $\implies C = 2{,}068\text{ lines/day}, q = 443\text{ lines/block}$, weekly capacity $14{,}770$ lines)** centers historical 2010 Q4 training utilization at **0.925** without touching validation or test data, allowing schedules to adapt dynamically across shoulder and peak weeks.

## 2. Empirical UCI Results (`results/uci/`, 12 Sealed Test Weeks: Sep 19 – Dec 9, 2011)
1. **Forecasting Accuracy (WAPE):** Nonnegative L2-regularized Ridge regression ($\alpha^* = 50.0$ selected on the 8-week rolling validation set) reduced validation WAPE from **32.65%** to **22.42%** (`-10.23 pp`) and sealed-test WAPE from **21.02%** (Previous-Week baseline) to **19.56%** (`-1.46 pp`).
2. **Primary Peak-Sized Staffing Performance (`Train P90`: $C = 2{,}068, q = 443, \lambda_{\text{Fri}} = 3$):**
   * **Flat Staffing (`2-2-2-2-2`):** `113,335` line-days, `22,230` Friday carryover lines, **`180,025`** total objective cost.
   * **Ridge Forecast + [Pyomo](https://pyomo.readthedocs.io/en/6.8.0/library_reference/appsi/appsi.html)/HiGHS MILP:** `82,279` line-days (`-27.4%`), `22,230` Friday lines (zero excess weekend spillover), **`148,969`** objective cost (**`+17.25%` improvement vs. Flat**; **10 wins, 0 ties, 2 losses**; 3-week moving-block bootstrap 95% CI `[+1,064, +4,232]` saved/week), dynamically selecting 5 distinct weekly profiles (`3-1-0-3-3`, `4-2-1-2-1`, `4-2-1-1-2`, `4-4-1-1-0`, `4-4-2-0-0`).
   * **Previous-Week Forecast + MILP:** `80,546` line-days, `22,349` Friday lines (`+119` extra Friday spillover), **`147,593`** objective cost (**`+18.02%` vs. Flat**; **9 wins, 0 ties, 3 losses**; Oracle ceiling **`142,268`** / `+20.97%`).
3. **Why Lower WAPE Does Not Automatically Maximize MILP Savings:** Under tighter seasonal capacity (`2010 Q4 Training Median`: $C = 1{,}740, q = 373$) and tighter block productivity (`0.75q`), **Ridge + MILP outperforms Previous-Week + MILP** (`+13.01%` vs. `+10.59%`, and `+12.72%` vs. `+10.88%` at `0.75q`). At higher capacity (`P90`/`P95`), Ridge's L2 shrinkage damps weekday extremes—preventing Week 3's `-632` naive loss (`0` backlog under Ridge!) and lowering weekly loss count (`2` vs. `3`), but shifting 1–2 fewer blocks to Monday/Tuesday during the sharp Weeks 7–8 November ramp (`Prev - Ridge` bootstrap 95% CI `[-696, +400]`/week, statistically indistinguishable).

## 3. Operational Recommendations
* **Align Seasonal Core Capacity ($C$) Before Tactical Flex Optimization:** Calibrate core headcount to seasonal training quantiles (`P85`–`P90` for peak quarters) so weekly utilization sits near $0.85\text{–}1.10$ where tactical flex routing adds `+13%` to `+18%` value over flat rosters.
* **Protect Shoulder Weeks Against Early-Week Over-Allocation:** Both MILP losses under `Train P90` occurred in late-September shoulder weeks (`W1`, `W2`) when forecasted queues emptied mid-week; adding a two-stage stochastic residual buffer or a 1-block mid-week floor mitigates shoulder-week forecast misses before live WMS pilot deployment.
